# 📊 Magnificent 7 Market Cap Dashboard

## Overview

This project explores how the market capitalization of the “Magnificent 7” tech companies has evolved over time. The goal was to build a clean, interactive Power BI dashboard that makes it easy to compare companies, track growth, and understand long-term trends from 2000 to 2024.

Rather than just presenting numbers, the focus was on creating a dashboard that tells a clear story about how these companies have grown and how their positions in the market have changed.

---

## What the Dashboard Does

* Tracks market capitalization across years for all seven companies
* Highlights year-over-year and previous period changes
* Allows filtering by company and time period
* Shows how each company contributes to overall market growth
* Visualizes ranking changes over time

---

## Key Takeaways

* NVIDIA stands out with rapid growth in recent years
* Apple and Microsoft show strong and consistent performance over time
* Tesla’s growth is significant but more volatile compared to others
* The combined market cap of these companies has grown substantially, especially after 2020
