# 📊 Magnificent 7 Market Cap Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes the market capitalization trends of the "Magnificent 7" tech companies — Apple, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Amazon, Google, Meta, and Tesla — from 2000 to 2024.

The dashboard provides interactive insights into growth trends, yearly comparisons, and company rankings.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* Dynamic KPI cards (Market Cap, YoY Growth, Previous Period)
* Interactive slicers for company and year selection
* Stacked expansion chart showing market cap growth
* Ranked trend visualization of company performance
* Advanced DAX calculations for time intelligence

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* NVIDIA shows explosive growth in recent years, especially 2024
* Apple and Microsoft maintain consistent market leadership
* Tesla exhibits high volatility compared to peers
* Overall market cap of the Magnificent 7 reached ~$16.95T in 2024
